    How to intercept

    1. Pick your app's tab above and click Attach.
    2. Turn Intercept ON.
    3. Use your app — requests pause here so you can edit, forward or drop them.

    Tip: for payment tests, open the extension popup and turn on Auto mode to force a parameter (e.g. amount) to a fixed value on every request — no manual editing needed.
